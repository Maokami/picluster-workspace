picluster Workspace

A personal, single-user daily briefing tool operated by Maokami.

The tool reads selected unread Gmail messages and calendar events to prepare a short daily summary for its owner. GitHub repository notifications are excluded from the mail summary.

Google access

Gmail and Google Calendar access is read-only. This integration does not send mail or create, edit, or delete calendar events. Google sign-in identifies the account that authorizes the integration.

How summaries are delivered

Selected content is processed through the owner's Claude account. The daily summary is delivered to the owner's configured Telegram chat and may be retained in local service logs.

This site provides information about the integration. It has no sign-up form and does not provide access to the owner's mailbox, calendar, credentials, or private operations repository.
